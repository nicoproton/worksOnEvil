# New Atlantis

***New Atlantis*** is Francis Bacon's unfinished utopian fiction, published posthumously in 1626. It describes the island commonwealth of Bensalem and, above all, Salomon's House: a state-supported scientific institution devoted to the organized investigation of nature. It is one of the most important positive ancestors of the scientific institute motif in the graph.

## Bensalem as Scientific Commonwealth

Bensalem is prosperous, pious, orderly, and selectively open to outsiders. Its society is not presented as anti-religious; science, Christian providence, and civic order appear integrated.

This makes the work an early anchor for [[Scientific World State]], though at island scale rather than planetary scale. Knowledge is institutionalized for public benefit.

## Salomon's House

Salomon's House is described as the "eye" of the kingdom, a research institution that collects knowledge, conducts experiments, develops technologies, and distinguishes true works of nature from imposture.

This gives the graph a new concept: [[Scientific Institute as World-Eye]]. Bacon imagines the research institution as a privileged organ of perception for the whole society.

## Experiment and Secrecy

Salomon's House does not reveal everything it knows. Its members decide which discoveries should be published and which should be kept back.

This connects the work to [[Elite Network Historiography]] and [[Layered Conspiracy]] in a careful way. Bacon's institution is not sinister in the text, but it establishes the structural possibility of expert knowledge withheld for governance.

## Beneficent Science and Later Inversion

The power of *New Atlantis* for this vault is that it is not dystopian. It gives the positive image that later works invert: the scientific institution as guardian of human welfare.

That makes it a necessary ancestor for *[[That Hideous Strength]]*. The N.I.C.E. is a dark parody of Salomon's House: a research institute that claims public benefit while becoming spiritually and politically corrupt.
