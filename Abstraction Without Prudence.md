# Abstraction Without Prudence

Abstraction Without Prudence names the pattern in which calculation, procedure, measurement, theory, or formal order replaces practical judgment about persons and situations. The failure is not that reason is used, but that reason becomes detached from the concrete goods it should serve.

This bridge belongs under [[Weaponized Rationalism]]. It is useful when a work emphasizes abstract rule, procedural logic, quantified judgment, or theoretical schemes that become coercive because they no longer answer to prudence, mercy, embodied life, or truth.

Use this link when the harm comes through a rational form that is internally coherent but humanly blind.

*[[Gulliver's Travels]]* gives the comic prehistory of the motif. Laputa and Lagado show theory swollen beyond proportion until rule, experiment, and improvement lose contact with ordinary life.

*[[The Trial (Kafka)]]* gives the bureaucratic nightmare form: procedure continues without intelligible accusation, justice, or mercy.

*[[We (Zamyatin)]]* gives the mathematical-political form. The One State makes life legible through glass, number, schedule, and surgery, then treats inward freedom as error.

*[[Psycho-Pass]]* gives the quantified cybernetic form. Moral judgment becomes technical output, and the system's apparent objectivity displaces responsibility.

*[[The Machine Stops]]* gives the disembodied comfort form. Rationalized mediation makes embodied life appear inefficient, embarrassing, and almost irrational.

*[[Cat's Cradle]]* gives the scientific-discovery form: ice-nine is elegant, technically astonishing, and catastrophic because invention outruns prudence. *[[Player Piano]]* gives the social-administrative form, where tests, machines, and managerial classification replace the concrete dignity of work. *[[Ratner's Star]]* gives the comic research-institute form, where mathematical brilliance does not automatically become wisdom.
