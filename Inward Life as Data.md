# Inward Life as Data

Inward Life as Data names works where memory, desire, attention, perception, personality, or consciousness becomes recordable, transferable, marketable, editable, or technically accessible. The danger is not merely that information exists about a person, but that the inner life itself becomes material for systems of commerce, governance, entertainment, therapy, or survival.

This bridge belongs near [[Mediated Reality]], [[Disembodied Persistence]], [[Media-Flesh Fusion]], and [[Corporate Sovereignty]]. It is useful when a work treats interface technology as a qualitative change in human experience rather than as a new gadget.

Use this link when:

- thoughts, memories, dreams, perceptions, or personalities can be technically captured;
- commerce or politics gains access to inward life rather than only outward behavior;
- identity becomes a transferable profile, recording, feed, copy, or media product;
- the body remains involved, but the soul-like interior is treated as data.

*[[Synners]]* gives the neural-media form: music video, creativity, infection, and corporate entertainment move through direct brain-computer interface. *[[The Fortunate Fall]]* gives the historical-media form: memory and perception become broadcast material through which public history is packaged. *[[Feed]]* gives the consumer form: advertising and networked language enter adolescent thought through implanted media. *[[Permutation City]]* gives the metaphysical-computational form: consciousness itself is treated as executable information. *[[Kaiba]]* gives the memory-body form: memory can be stored, stolen, transferred, sold, and used to detach identity from bodily continuity.

This concept differs from [[Simulated Reality]]. A simulation supplies an environment; inward life as data concerns the capture or conversion of the person's interior. It differs from [[Engineered Consent]] because the issue is not only persuasion, but access to the substrate of perception, memory, and desire.
