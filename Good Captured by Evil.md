# Good Captured by Evil

Good Captured by Evil names stories where a real good is redirected into destructive service. Restoration, healing, knowledge, order, beauty, peace, belonging, or power may be genuinely desirable, but becomes dangerous when it is captured by a false end.

This bridge belongs near [[False Salvation Through Systems]] and [[Privatio Boni]]. It is especially useful when evil does not invent its own good, but parasitizes a prior good and turns it toward domination, possession, or false completion.

Use this link when the text makes the goodness of the captured thing important. If the supposed good is only a disguise with no real value, a different concept may be clearer.

In *[[Terranigma]]*, Ark's restoration of the world is real, yet Dark Gaia tries to route that restoration into a destructive cycle. In *[[That Hideous Strength]]*, public reform, science, and institutional repair are captured by demonic and technocratic domination. In *[[Nausicaa of the Valley of the Wind (Manga)]]*, the Crypt's promise of restoration is captured by a dead civilization's will to judge and replace the living world. In *[[The Gods Are Athirst]]*, justice and civic virtue are captured by the Terror's demand for purity.

This concept is a useful guard against flattening stories into "system bad." The strongest works often show why the system attracts loyalty: it has attached itself to something genuinely good.
