# Global Problematique

Global Problematique names the idea that modern crises form an interdependent system rather than a set of separate problems. Environment, population, development, technology, security, economics, and governance interact at planetary scale.

## Systems Crisis

The concept is useful when a work or theory treats crisis as systemic. No single local reform is enough because each problem is linked to others through feedback loops and institutions.

This connects to [[Managed Crisis]] and [[Scientific World State]]. Systemic crises can be real, but their proposed solutions often require larger systems of coordination and control.

## Crisis as Unifier

The concept becomes especially important when crisis is used to generate consent. A shared threat can motivate cooperation, but it can also justify managed consciousness, emergency governance, and expert rule.

This links the concept to [[World-Consciousness Formation]] and [[Elite Network Historiography]].

*[[The First Global Revolution]]* is the main anchor for the term in this vault. Wells's world-state texts and Huxley's UNESCO philosophy are adjacent because they also treat crisis as requiring planetary consciousness and institutional coordination.
