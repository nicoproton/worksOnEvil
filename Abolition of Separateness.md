# Abolition of Separateness

Abolition of Separateness names the promise of healing loneliness, conflict, or incompleteness by dissolving the boundaries between persons. It belongs under [[Forced Human Completion]] when the cure for human pain is not relation between distinct persons, but the removal of distinction itself.

Use this link for:

- Projects that merge persons into a collective, sea, network, or single psychic field.
- Salvation through the end of privacy, distance, individuality, or embodied separateness.
- Systems that treat loneliness as proof that personhood itself is defective.
- Stories where love is replaced by absorption.

*[[Neon Genesis Evangelion]]* is the clearest case through Human Instrumentality: the wound of individuality is healed by abolishing individuality. *[[Serial Experiments Lain]]* gives a networked version, where connection threatens to become a godlike function that erases the separateness needed for love and memory.

This concept is the negative mirror of [[Embodied Relation]] and [[Fragile Counter-Community]]. Those concepts preserve persons in relation; abolition of separateness removes the person in order to secure unity.
