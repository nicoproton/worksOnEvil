# Black Iron Prison

Black Iron Prison is Philip K. Dick's image for a false world of domination that persists beneath apparent history. In *[[VALIS]]*, the Roman Empire has not ended; the prison is a recurring structure of power, illusion, and spiritual captivity.

## Gnostic Prison

The concept belongs near [[Gnosticism and its Critics]]. The visible world may be a trap, and awakening requires the reception of hidden truth.

## Empire as Overlay

The Black Iron Prison is not only a political empire. It is an overlay on perception and time, making historical change appear real while the same domination continues.

This connects the concept to [[Simulated Reality]] and [[Mediated Reality]]. The prison is maintained by what people take to be reality.
