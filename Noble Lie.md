# Noble Lie

Noble Lie names a politically useful falsehood or myth told to stabilize a social order. The concept comes from Plato's *[[The Republic]]*, especially the myth of metals, where citizens are taught that their social roles correspond to different metals in their souls.

## Myth as Social Glue

The noble lie is not mere propaganda in the crude sense. It is a founding story meant to make people accept their place in the whole.

This links it to [[Engineered Consent]] and [[Language as Control System]]. Political language does not only persuade; it can found a shared reality.

## Dangerous Medicine

The concept is dangerous because it subordinates truth to social harmony. If the rulers decide which falsehoods are noble, truth becomes an instrument of governance.

This connects the concept to [[False Salvation Through Systems]]. A system may claim that deception is necessary to preserve justice, peace, or unity.
