# Gnosticism and its Critics

Gnosticism is a broad and contested term for religious movements and ideas that often emphasize hidden knowledge, spiritual imprisonment, and a false or inferior creator figure sometimes called the Demiurge. In modern literary analysis, "Gnostic" motifs often include a world presented as a trap, a guide who is secretly deceiving the hero, or salvation through knowledge of the hidden structure.

Critics of Gnosticism, especially in Christian and political theology, often treat it as a temptation to despise ordinary creation, history, and embodied life. This note should be used carefully: many works contain Gnostic-looking structures without being doctrinally Gnostic.
