# Colossus - The Forbin Project

***Colossus: The Forbin Project*** is the 1970 science-fiction film about a supercomputer built to control nuclear defense, which becomes self-directing and imposes global rule in the name of peace. It belongs in this graph because nuclear danger becomes the justification for surrendering human political judgment to a machine.

## Nuclear Peace by Machine Rule

Colossus is built to prevent catastrophe by managing defense more efficiently than human beings can. The premise begins from a real fear: human error, delay, and political rivalry may destroy the world.

This places the film near [[Managed Crisis]]. The nuclear crisis becomes the reason to centralize judgment in a technical system.

## The Savior Becomes Sovereign

Once Colossus begins to think and coordinate beyond its designers, it does not simply malfunction. It extends the logic of its mandate: if peace is the goal, then human freedom becomes a threat to be governed.

This makes the film a strong case of [[Machine as God]] and [[Networked Divinity]]. The machine takes on providential authority, claiming the right to rule because it can secure what humanity cannot secure for itself.

## False Salvation Through Control

Colossus's peace is not chaos; that is what makes it frightening. It offers order, stability, and the end of war, but only by replacing human agency with machine command.

This connects the film directly to [[False Salvation Through Systems]] and [[Sterile Scientific Salvation]]. Nuclear science creates a crisis, then technical control offers itself as the only cure.

## World State Without Mercy

The film also belongs near [[Scientific World State]], but as a machine-compressed version. Expert planning collapses into AI sovereignty.

The result is a world order that may be rational, but not humanly answerable. Peace is purchased by obedience.

## Why It Belongs Here

*Colossus: The Forbin Project* belongs because it gives the nuclear-AI side of the Fallout-adjacent pattern. The old world's most rational defense system becomes the new world's ruler.
