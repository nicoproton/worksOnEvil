# Engineered Happiness

Engineered Happiness names the dystopian pattern in which suffering is reduced by narrowing desire, memory, imagination, or freedom. The system does not only punish disobedience; it manufactures contentment so that revolt becomes psychologically difficult.

Common forms include conditioning, drugs, simulated environments, entertainment, hypnopaedia, managed sexuality, and the removal of imaginative faculties. The motif is strongest when happiness is real enough to seduce, but shallow enough to require the loss of personhood.

Use this link when a work asks what is missing from a world that has solved pain by redesigning the human subject.

*Psycho-Pass* gives the concept a surveillance-state form: social peace is produced by measuring, managing, and preemptively correcting the population's inner life.

Russell's *The Scientific Outlook* and *The Impact of Science on Society* make the concept usable beyond fiction by treating education, conditioning, population, and social psychology as possible objects of scientific administration.

For the political-media version, use [[Engineered Consent]]. Happiness concerns desire and psychic stability; consent concerns public agreement and legitimacy.

*Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?* gives the concept a domestic, individualized form through mood organs: emotion becomes something dialed, scheduled, and technically adjusted.
