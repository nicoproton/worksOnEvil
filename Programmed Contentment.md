# Programmed Contentment

Programmed Contentment names systems that produce social stability by shaping desire, mood, habit, fear, or imagination before open dissent can form. The goal is not merely obedience, but a population whose inner life has been adapted to the order around it.

This bridge belongs under [[Engineered Happiness]]. It is useful when happiness or peace is produced through education, conditioning, psychological measurement, caste formation, therapy, or social planning.

Use this link when a work focuses on the manufacture of stable subjects rather than on pleasure alone.

*[[Brave New World]]* is the central fictional case: hypnopaedia, caste conditioning, soma, and social ritual produce people who are largely unable to desire beyond the World State.

*[[Psycho-Pass]]* gives the surveillance form. Social peace depends on continuous measurement and correction of inner life.

Russell's *[[The Scientific Outlook]]*, *[[The Impact of Science on Society]]*, and *[[Education and the Social Order]]* provide non-fiction context because they treat education, psychology, population, and social behavior as possible fields of scientific administration.

*[[A Modern Utopia]]* and *[[New Bottles for New Wine]]* are more ambiguous cases. They are not simple dystopias, but they turn human development into something planned by elites, institutions, or evolutionary humanism.

*[[The Thanatos Syndrome]]* gives the medical-population form: contentment and stability are pursued by chemically narrowing the person, so social peace becomes a symptom of reduced humanity.
