# Cosmic Axis Under Siege

Cosmic Axis Under Siege names the pattern in which a world-center, sacred axis, or metaphysical structure holds reality together and becomes the target of destructive forces. The central image may be a tower, tree, mountain, road, beam, throne, or city, but its meaning is not architectural height. It is ordered participation in reality.

This concept should be distinguished from [[Tower of Babel Archetype]]. Babel is an upward project of usurpation: human or antichrist ambition tries to seize divine order from below. The cosmic axis pattern is different. The axis is already the legitimate center, and evil tries to damage, corrupt, occupy, or unmake it.

The motif often connects to [[Entropy and the Binding Force]], because the axis is what keeps worlds, communities, languages, histories, or beings from falling into dissolution. It connects to [[Privatio Boni]] only when evil cannot create a true center of its own and therefore attacks the given order that sustains it. If evil is presented as an opposed cosmic substance, [[Manichaeism and Zoroastrianism]] may be the better link.

Use this link when the drama concerns preserving, healing, or defending the structure that binds reality, not constructing a rival ascent to heaven.

*[[Dark Earth]]* gives the motif a local and civic form. Sparta's Well or source of Light is not a tower of usurpation, but the sustaining center whose destruction would expose the city to darkness.
