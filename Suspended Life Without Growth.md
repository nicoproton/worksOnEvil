# Suspended Life Without Growth

Suspended Life Without Growth names the false preservation of life by freezing, isolating, fragmenting, or technically maintaining it after ordinary growth and relation have been removed. It belongs under [[Artificial Immortality]] when survival is purchased by losing the living movement that made survival worth wanting.

This bridge is useful because some works do not imagine immortality as triumph. They imagine it as storage: a remnant, head, body, or population kept from death while also kept from flourishing.

Use this link for:

- Bodies preserved without ordinary agency, growth, or relation.
- Medical or technical survival that becomes a parody of life.
- Chosen remnants saved by freezing, suspension, or depersonalizing preservation.
- Cure from death that produces a controlled half-life.

*[[Terranigma]]* is a strong case through Beruga's suspended remnant and zombie-like immortality. *[[That Hideous Strength]]* gives the pattern a grotesque emblem in the severed Head, where technical preservation becomes an oracle for powers deeper than the scientists understand.

This bridge often overlaps with [[False Cure]] when survival is offered as healing, and with [[Ritual Technology]] when the preserved body becomes a medium for hidden powers.
