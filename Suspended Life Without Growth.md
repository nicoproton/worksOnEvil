# Suspended Life Without Growth

Suspended Life Without Growth names the false preservation of life by freezing, isolating, fragmenting, or technically maintaining it after ordinary growth and relation have been removed. It also names social or metaphysical stasis when a system promises life by preventing change. It belongs under [[Artificial Immortality]] when survival is purchased by losing the living movement that made survival worth wanting.

This bridge is useful because some works do not imagine immortality as triumph. They imagine it as storage: a remnant, head, body, or population kept from death while also kept from flourishing.

Use this link for:

- Bodies preserved without ordinary agency, growth, or relation.
- Medical or technical survival that becomes a parody of life.
- Chosen remnants saved by freezing, suspension, or depersonalizing preservation.
- Cure from death that produces a controlled half-life.
- "Unchanging world" projects where safety or immortality requires the end of history, risk, succession, or renewal.

*[[Terranigma]]* is a strong case through Beruga's suspended remnant and his dream of eternal life in an unchanging world. The contrast with Lhasa's wheel of life makes the point clear: death, change, and recurrence are not simply defects to be eliminated. *[[That Hideous Strength]]* gives the pattern a grotesque emblem in the severed Head, where technical preservation becomes an oracle for powers deeper than the scientists understand.

The bridge sharpens into [[False Apotheosis]] when suspended life is not only preservation but the bait of elite transformation: the chosen expect to survive beyond ordinary humanity, while the system or hidden power treats them as usable remains.

This bridge often overlaps with [[False Cure]] when survival is offered as healing, and with [[Ritual Technology]] when the preserved body becomes a medium for hidden powers.
