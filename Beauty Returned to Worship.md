# Beauty Returned to Worship

Beauty Returned to Worship names the pattern in which aesthetic hunger is purified by being redirected toward prayer, liturgy, sacrament, sacred architecture, chant, monastic discipline, or contemplative order. It is not a rejection of beauty. It is a refusal to let beauty remain private sensation, occult fascination, or decadent enclosure.

This bridge belongs near [[Beauty from Corruption]], [[Aesthetic Self-Enclosure]], and [[Enchantment vs Magic]]. It marks the counter-movement: beauty no longer seals the self inside its own intensity, but becomes a way of receiving reality as gift.

Use this link when:

- decadent or aesthetic sensitivity is redirected toward worship;
- art, symbol, music, or architecture becomes a path of conversion rather than self-display;
- ritual is restored from inversion into sacrament, prayer, or discipline;
- the work treats beauty as ordered participation in the good.

Huysmans's Durtal cycle is the clearest cluster. *[[Against Nature (A Rebours)]]* gives the enclosed decadent form, *[[La-bas (Down There)]]* passes through occult inversion, *[[En route]]* turns toward confession and monastic discipline, *[[La Cathedrale]]* reads Chartres as symbolic Catholic cosmos, and *[[L'Oblat]]* tests whether conversion can become stable rule of life.

This concept is not the same as [[Sacred Infrastructure]]. A cathedral, monastery, or liturgy is not sacred because it functions as a life-support system. It is sacred because it orders matter, time, art, and community toward worship.
