# The Brothers Karamazov

***The Brothers Karamazov*** is Fyodor Dostoevsky's final novel, published in 1880. In this vault, its most important section is the "Grand Inquisitor" chapter, where Ivan Karamazov imagines Christ returning during the Spanish Inquisition and being rejected by a religious ruler who believes humanity prefers bread, miracle, authority, and security to freedom.

That argument clarifies several notes on ideological control: domination can present itself as mercy when it promises to remove the burden of freedom.
