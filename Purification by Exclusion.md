# Purification by Exclusion

Purification by Exclusion names systems that promise peace, safety, health, holiness, or order by identifying some form of life as impurity and removing it. The excluded may be criminals, dissenters, the sick, the irregular, the unfit, the unclean, or beings whose personhood the system refuses to recognize.

This bridge belongs under [[False Salvation Through Systems]]. The system appears salvific because it offers relief from fear or disorder, but it achieves purity by narrowing the community of beings who count.

Use this link when a system's cure depends on deletion, elimination, quarantine, psychic cleansing, social sorting, or the denial of personhood.

In *[[Psycho-Pass]]*, cloudy interiority is treated as civic pollution. In *[[Shin Megami Tensei IV (Video Game)]]*, the Law vision offers peace through the removal of impurity, dissent, and concealed history. In *[[BLAME!]]*, the Safeguard treats unauthorized humans as illegitimate life. In *[[Digimon (Franchise)]]*, the D-Reaper treats emergent digital life as excess data to be deleted. In *[[Fallout]]*, the Enclave treats most postwar humanity as genetic contamination rather than as the people to be saved. In *[[The Gods Are Athirst]]*, revolutionary virtue becomes a demand to expose and remove enemies of the people. In *[[The 120 Days of Sodom]]*, the libertine enclosure defines victims as beings outside moral protection.

This concept is not identical to [[Privatio Boni]]. It describes a social or systemic mechanism of exclusion, not an ontology of evil.
