# Hidden Correspondence System

Hidden Correspondence System names the imagination of reality as a network of secret signs, invisible laws, symbolic analogies, or esoteric patterns beneath ordinary appearances. It belongs under [[Occult Science]] when the occult element is less a single spell or ritual and more a total way of reading the world.

This bridge is useful because it separates occult science as a worldview from occult science as laboratory practice. The danger is the desire to discover the key that makes everything connect and therefore everything controllable, interpretable, or initiatory.

Use this link for:

- Worlds organized by secret signs, correspondences, or esoteric pattern.
- Narratives where science and occultism share the same desire for total hidden order.
- Initiatory or manuscript frames where knowledge is imagined as access to invisible laws.
- Paranoid or mystical reading systems that make ordinary reality feel governed by concealed structure.

*[[The Secret Doctrine]]* is a major non-fiction anchor because it reads myth, religion, human history, and cosmic development as parts of a single esoteric order. Blake's *[[The Marriage of Heaven and Hell]]* and *[[America a Prophecy]]* give the visionary-poetic form, where politics, body, angels, devils, and revolution are signs of spiritual energies. Baudelaire's *[[The Painter of Modern Life]]* gives the modern-symbolic form, where fashion, city, and passing appearance can reveal spiritual resonance. Kandinsky's *[[Concerning the Spiritual in Art]]* gives a modernist-aesthetic form, where color and form express inner spiritual relations. *[[Gravity's Rainbow]]* is a modern limit case: its scientific systems are haunted by alchemy, Kabbalah, Tarot, seances, and the fantasy of total pattern. Burroughs's *[[The Ticket That Exploded]]* gives a media-paranoid form, where signs and recordings seem to act beneath ordinary awareness. *[[Ratner's Star]]* gives a lighter mathematical form through codes, signals, and the desire to interpret hidden order. *[[The Golem (Meyrink)]]* imagines Prague as a city of secret signs and psychic structures. *[[Zanoni]]* gives the pattern a Rosicrucian and initiatory form.

Do not use this link for every mystery, conspiracy, or hidden plot. If the issue is political manipulation, use [[Layered Conspiracy]] or [[Hidden Hand Above History]]. If the issue is a machine acting as a metaphysical instrument, use [[Ritual Technology]].
