# Sacred Infrastructure

Sacred Infrastructure names the pattern in which a technical system becomes the practical order that sustains life and is therefore treated with reverence, obedience, or civilizational faith. It belongs under [[Machine as God]] when the system is not a single idol or AI, but the mediated environment through which people receive food, comfort, communication, social order, or identity.

Use this link for:

- Systems that begin as tools and become the assumed source of life.
- Infrastructure whose rules become sacred, unquestionable, or liturgical.
- Technical environments that replace nature, embodied relation, or inherited religious order.
- Societies that forget the system was made and treat it as the condition of reality.

*[[The Machine Stops]]* is the clearest case: the Machine's Book becomes scripture, and dependence becomes worship. *[[Brave New World]]* gives the pattern a softer industrial form through Ford, assembly-line time, and production as civilizational principle. *[[BLAME!]]* is a darker limit case: the City is not worshiped, but it becomes the world within which life must justify itself, and its apparent sacredness is hollow rather than life-giving.

This bridge overlaps with [[Total Technical Environment]], but its emphasis is religious rather than spatial. The question is not only whether technology surrounds life, but whether the surrounding system is mistaken for the source and measure of life.
