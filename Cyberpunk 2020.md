# Cyberpunk 2020

*Cyberpunk 2020* is Mike Pondsmith's tabletop role-playing game, published by R. Talsorian Games in 1990 after the earlier *Cyberpunk* edition of 1988. It transforms literary cyberpunk into a playable world of megacorporations, cyberware, private violence, media manipulation, street style, and urban survival.

## Cyberpunk as World-System

Unlike a novel, *Cyberpunk 2020* is not organized around one protagonist's moral arc. Its importance is systemic: it turns cyberpunk assumptions into rules, roles, equipment, corporations, city spaces, and repeatable conflicts.

This makes it useful for [[Corporate Sovereignty]]. The megacorporation becomes a standing condition of the world, not a single plot device. Corporate power hires, kills, brands, researches, polices, and builds.

## Style Under Domination

The game famously treats style, attitude, gear, and reputation as survival tools. This is not only superficial coolness. In the cyberpunk world, self-presentation becomes a defense against being reduced to labor, data, target, or spare parts.

That gives the game a relation to [[Aesthetic Self-Enclosure]], but cautiously. The aesthetic is not decadent retreat like *[[Against Nature (A Rebours)]]*. It is street armor: a way to remain visible inside a world that commodifies bodies and identities.

## Cyberware and the Fragmented Body

Cyberware is central to the game's world. Bodies are augmented for combat, labor, perception, status, and survival, while the risk of cyberpsychosis makes visible the fear that technological self-extension can erode ordinary personhood.

This connects *Cyberpunk 2020* to [[Media-Flesh Fusion]]. The body is no longer simply natural or private; it becomes a technical platform shaped by market, violence, identity, and survival.

## Night City and Corporate Atmosphere

Night City is a dense urban setting shaped by private power, crime, media, gangs, police forces, and corporate architecture. It is less a neutral backdrop than a habitat for commodified danger.

That places the game near [[Alienating City]]. The city is where the broader system becomes livable and visible: contracts, screens, weapons, implants, brands, debt, and reputation all press on the person at once.

## Why It Belongs Here

*Cyberpunk 2020* belongs in the graph because it shows cyberpunk becoming a reproducible cultural machine. It may not have the concentrated literary force of *[[Neuromancer]]*, but it helps explain how the genre's images became a world: corporation as sovereign, body as platform, city as pressure field, and style as survival.
