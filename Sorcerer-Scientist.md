# Sorcerer-Scientist

Sorcerer-Scientist names the figure whose technical expertise is narrated as a form of magical or occult mastery. The laboratory becomes a workshop of conjuration, not because the work rejects science, but because invention is entangled with possession, imitation, revenge, erotic projection, or the desire to animate what should not be animated.

This bridge belongs under [[Occult Science]] when the emphasis falls on a maker, inventor, or experimenter whose knowledge crosses from technique into spiritual danger.

Use this link for:

- Inventors whose work resembles conjuration or animation.
- Laboratories marked as ritual, alchemical, uncanny, or spiritually dangerous spaces.
- Artificial beings made through a fusion of scientific apparatus and older magical desire.
- Technical mastery that exposes the maker's disordered loves.

It is strongest in *[[Metropolis (Novel)]]*, where Rotwang's modern machinery is housed inside an occult space and driven by revenge. It also clarifies *[[The Future Eve]]*, where Edison becomes a magician of modern invention, and *[[The Sandman (Hoffmann)]]*, where optics, automata, alchemy, and demonic suggestion blur around Coppelius, Coppola, and Olympia.

Do not use it for every scientist, technocrat, or inventor. If the institution is the main danger, use bridges such as [[Scientific World State]] or [[Respectable Front]]. If the machine itself functions as a metaphysical apparatus, [[Ritual Technology]] may be more precise.
