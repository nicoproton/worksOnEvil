# Holarchy

Holarchy names Arthur Koestler's model of nested wholes and parts. A holon is both a whole in itself and a part of a larger whole. A healthy order preserves both dimensions: selfhood and integration.

## Against Reduction

The concept resists reducing persons either to isolated atoms or to mere parts of a collective. A person is a whole, but also belongs to families, communities, institutions, ecologies, and histories.

This makes holarchy useful against [[Abolition of Separateness]]. Systems that promise perfect unity often destroy the whole-person dimension by reducing persons to parts.

## Against Fragmentation

Holarchy also resists pure individualism. If the integrative dimension collapses, persons and societies become fragmented, unable to participate in larger goods.

This connects the concept to [[Entropy and the Binding Force]]. Binding force is not absorption into the collective, but healthy integration across levels of life.
