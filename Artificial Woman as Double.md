# Artificial Woman as Double

Artificial Woman as Double names the motif of a manufactured or animated female figure who replaces, imitates, or distorts a living woman. The double often becomes a screen for desire, fear, idealization, and social disorder.

This concept should be used when artificial life is specifically gendered and mimetic: not just a made being, but a woman-shaped copy, idol, substitute, or false image. It overlaps with [[Golem Tradition]] and [[Occult Science]] when animation carries spiritual or ritual force.

The motif runs from Olympia in *[[The Sandman (Hoffmann)]]* through Hadaly in *[[The Future Eve]]* and false Maria in *[[Metropolis (Novel)]]*. Later works can transform the same figure from projection into a morally visible manufactured person, as with Rachael in *[[Blade Runner]]*.
