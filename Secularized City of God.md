# Secularized City of God

Secularized City of God names the pattern in which Christian historical hope, providence, pilgrimage, ecclesial destiny, or the heavenly city are translated into immanent projects of historical salvation. The city remains, but its center changes: history, nation, humanity, class, progress, science, or political order takes over the role once held by God.

This bridge belongs near [[Progress as Secular Providence]], [[Religion of Humanity]], and [[False Salvation Through Systems]]. It is useful when a work does not merely praise progress, but reshapes Christian eschatological structure into a worldly program.

Use this link when:

- Christian hope is translated into historical progress or political fulfillment;
- the heavenly city becomes a social, national, revolutionary, scientific, or humanitarian project;
- providence is retained as historical direction after God is removed or subordinated;
- politics or culture promises a final unity that belongs properly to divine salvation.

Etienne Gilson's *[[The Metamorphoses of the City of God]]* is the central historical-philosophical anchor. Gilson follows the afterlives of Augustine's City of God as later thought transforms Christian historical meaning into other forms.

*[[Progress and Religion]]* gives a Catholic historical neighbor. Dawson shows how progress can become a civilizational faith.

*[[Spe Salvi]]* gives the magisterial theological boundary: science, structures, and politics can help human life, but they cannot redeem man or replace Christian hope.

Comte's *[[Catechism of Positive Religion]]* gives an explicit Religion-of-Humanity form. Wells's [[Scientific World State]] gives the planning-scientific form. Marxist and revolutionary forms appear through [[Atheistic Communism]] and [[Progress as Secular Providence]].

This concept should not be used for every utopia or every political theory. It belongs where a Christian pattern of salvation-history has been secularized, immanentized, or politically transformed.
