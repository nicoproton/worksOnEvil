# Regensburg Address

***Regensburg Address*** is Benedict XVI's 2006 university lecture, formally titled "Faith, Reason and the University: Memories and Reflections." It belongs in this graph because it gives one of his clearest public arguments against the narrowing of reason to empirical method, technique, and historical reduction.

The address became historically controversial because of its quotation concerning Islam and violence. This note does not use it as a node about Islam. Its graph value is the larger argument: faith and reason belong together, and a culture that narrows reason loses the capacity to judge religion, violence, ethics, and itself.

## Logos Against Violence

Benedict's central theological claim is that not acting according to reason is contrary to God's nature. God is not arbitrary will or sheer force. Christian faith is bound to Logos, and therefore cannot make violence into a true instrument of faith.

This gives the address a strong relation to [[Reason Ordered to Truth]]. Reason is not external to faith as a secular policeman. It belongs inside the Christian confession of God as Logos.

## The Narrowing of Modern Reason

The address's most important modern diagnosis is the reduction of reason to what can be mathematically, empirically, or technically verified. That reduced reason is powerful, but it excludes the questions of God, ethics, meaning, and finality from public rationality.

This connects the lecture to [[Weaponized Rationalism]]. Benedict is not attacking science. He is warning that when science's method becomes the whole definition of reason, man himself is reduced.

It also strengthens [[Theological Modernism]] at one precise point: theology can survive as history, ethics, or religious studies while losing its claim to speak rationally about God.

## Dehellenization

Benedict names several waves of dehellenization: attempts to separate Christianity from the Greek philosophical inheritance that helped articulate Logos, being, reason, and truth.

This makes the address a companion to [[Dogmatic Relativism]] and [[Revelation Under Critical Reduction]]. If metaphysical language is treated as a disposable cultural shell, doctrine can be reinterpreted until the stable truth it carries becomes thin.

## Why It Belongs Here

The *Regensburg Address* is one of the cleanest Catholic counter-nodes to the graph's technocratic and relativist branches. It insists that the cure for modern irrationality is not less reason, but broader reason: reason open to God, being, ethics, beauty, history, and the whole question of man.
