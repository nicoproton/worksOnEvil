# Corrected Inwardness

Corrected Inwardness names systems that treat imagination, moral resistance, desire, anxiety, criminal potential, grief, or spiritual hunger as defects to be measured, medicated, corrected, or removed. It belongs under [[Forced Human Completion]] when the system claims to perfect the person by narrowing the interior life that makes freedom and relation possible.

Use this link for:

- Surgery, therapy, conditioning, or measurement aimed at removing inner freedom.
- Systems that define imagination, grief, anxiety, opacity, or moral unpredictability as civic problems.
- Social peace produced by making people less inwardly capable of dissent.
- False cures where ordinary personhood is treated as the disease.

*[[We (Zamyatin)]]* gives the surgical form through the Great Operation, which removes imagination. *[[Psycho-Pass]]* gives the measured and therapeutic form through the Psycho-Pass and Crime Coefficient. *[[Brave New World]]* gives the soft-conditioning form: unwanted depth is prevented before it can become resistance.

*[[The Thanatos Syndrome]]* gives a Catholic medical-satirical form. The population is chemically adjusted toward manageability, but the treated person becomes less morally and spiritually serious rather than healed.

Illich's *[[Medical Nemesis]]* is a non-fiction neighbor when ordinary suffering, fear, aging, or dependency is absorbed into medical categories that weaken the person's capacity to endure and interpret his own condition.

This concept overlaps with [[Programmed Contentment]] when inner life is shaped toward stability, and with [[False Cure]] when the correction is presented as healing.
