# The Abolition of Man

***The Abolition of Man*** is C.S. Lewis's 1943 work of moral and educational criticism. Lewis argues that modern attempts to debunk value judgments as mere subjective feeling risk producing "men without chests": people whose intellect and appetite are no longer governed by trained moral affections.

The work is important for notes on [[Weaponized Rationalism]] and [[Scientific World State]] because Lewis links the conquest of nature to the conquest of humanity. If moral law is treated as conditioning, then the people with technical power become conditioners of everyone else.
