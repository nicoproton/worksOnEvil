# Secular Antichrist Utopia

Secular Antichrist Utopia names a modern apocalyptic pattern in which peace, unity, humanitarian compassion, or rational world order becomes the vehicle for spiritual domination. The danger is not open chaos but a false harmony that requires the rejection of transcendent truth.

This concept should be used carefully. It belongs to explicitly theological or apocalyptic works, especially those where a charismatic world-unifier or institution offers salvation while displacing God with humanity, progress, or the system itself.
