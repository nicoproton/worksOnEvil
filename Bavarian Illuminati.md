# Bavarian Illuminati

The **Bavarian Illuminati** was an Enlightenment-era secret society founded by Adam Weishaupt in 1776. It became a major object of counter-revolutionary suspicion after the French Revolution, especially in works that claimed secret societies had directed revolutionary upheaval.

In this vault, the Illuminati are most relevant through Abbé Barruel's *[[Memoirs Illustrating the History of Jacobinism]]*, where they serve as the innermost layer of a supposed anti-Christian, anti-monarchical, and anti-social conspiracy.
