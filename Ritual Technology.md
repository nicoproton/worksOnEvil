# Ritual Technology

Ritual Technology names the pattern in which a machine, experiment, or technical system behaves like a ritual apparatus. It mediates hidden powers, souls, bodies, memories, cosmic forces, or metaphysical thresholds while retaining the outward language of science, engineering, medicine, or military procedure.

This bridge belongs under [[Occult Science]] when the emphasis is not simply on an occult scientist, but on technology becoming the instrument through which invisible or spiritual powers are contacted, channeled, embodied, or forced into action.

Use this link for:

- Machines animated by souls, bodies, divine fragments, or non-human powers.
- Experiments that function as rites of contact, summoning, resurrection, revelation, or transformation.
- Technical systems whose operators misunderstand the metaphysical powers they are using.
- Scientific apparatus that becomes a threshold between ordinary life and hidden command.

*[[Neon Genesis Evangelion]]* is a central case because the Evangelions are technological, biological, maternal, and sacred at once. *[[Xenogears]]* uses machinery, ritual, prophecy, and false religion around Deus. *[[Revival (Stephen King)]]* turns electrical experiment into contact with a forbidden beyond. *[[That Hideous Strength]]* uses technical preservation and institutional science as a threshold through which fallen powers speak.

*[[eXistenZ]]* gives a secular media version: the organic game pod is a technical apparatus that opens a bodily threshold into a shared reality-system.

If the deeper power is explicitly infernal and commands a respectable front, [[Demonic Conspiracy]] should remain the sharper bridge. If the central figure is an inventor-magician, use [[Sorcerer-Scientist]].
