# Cosmic Law and Chaos

Cosmic Law and Chaos names stories where metaphysical or political order is divided between rival principles such as Law, Chaos, Neutrality, divine order, demonic freedom, hierarchy, dissolution, or world-remaking will.

This concept is not the same as [[Manichaeism and Zoroastrianism]]. Law and Chaos are not always good and evil, and the conflict may be ideological rather than moral. It is also distinct from [[Privatio Boni]], because the opposing principles are usually treated as real powers or cosmic options rather than evil as lack.
