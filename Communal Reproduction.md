# Communal Reproduction

Communal Reproduction names the motif in which reproduction, family, pairing, or child-rearing is made a public matter governed by the city, state, or system. The future human is treated as a civic product rather than the fruit of private family life.

## Utopian Origins

The concept appears in ancient and early-modern utopias such as *[[The Republic]]* and *[[The City of the Sun]]*. In both, the reproductive order is connected to the stability and quality of the whole city.

## From Reproduction to Manufacture

The concept is an ancestor of [[Manufactured Humanity]]. Later works replace civic pairing or controlled family life with hatcheries, cloning, artificial bodies, and genetic design.
