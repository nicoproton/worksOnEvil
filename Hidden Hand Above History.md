# Hidden Hand Above History

Hidden Hand Above History names works where visible politics, wars, religions, economies, or social conflicts are manipulated by a concealed higher order. The hidden order may be an empire, committee, faction, AI, occult power, alien power, or secret civilization.

This bridge belongs under [[Layered Conspiracy]]. It is useful when the main revelation is vertical: what looked like ordinary historical conflict is actually governed from above, beneath, or behind.

Use this link when the hidden structure does not merely infiltrate society, but reframes the apparent history of the world.

In *[[Xenogears]]*, Solaris manipulates surface nations while treating their peoples as material. In *[[Shin Megami Tensei IV (Video Game)]]*, Mikado's sacred surface conceals dependence on buried Tokyo and angelic information control. In *[[Neon Genesis Evangelion]]*, NERV, SEELE, and Gendo pursue different hidden outcomes behind the visible war for humanity's survival.

This concept should be used carefully for non-fiction. It names a narrative structure, not a license to turn every elite network into a master plot.
