# Parasitic World-Drainer

Parasitic World-Drainer names a force that feeds on the life, energy, or history of an entire world. Unlike an ordinary tyrant or machine, this figure treats creation itself as fuel.

The motif can be literal, as with a planetary parasite, or spiritual, as with a destructive power that uses restored life for its own continuation. It connects strongly to [[Privatio Boni]] only when the parasite cannot create its own good, only consume the vitality of another order.

If the parasite is framed as a rival cosmic substance or as part of a dualist metaphysics, the privation reading should be stated as an external argument or avoided.

When the draining is mediated through a machine, empire, or technical system, it overlaps with [[Stolen Life-Force Technology]].

Vampire and vampire-like works can miniaturize the pattern. *[[It (Stephen King)]]* does not drain a planet, but Derry becomes a local world organized around the feeding cycle of a parasitic intelligence.
