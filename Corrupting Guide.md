# Corrupting Guide

The corrupting guide is a figure who leads another character toward harm while appearing as mentor, patron, liberator, expert, or authority. The guide may lie openly, reveal only partial truths, or manipulate real desires toward destructive ends.

This motif is strongest when the guided character is not simply foolish. The deception works because it attaches itself to something good: the wish to know, belong, heal, restore, or serve.

Use this link when guidance itself becomes the instrument of corruption.

Sauron's role in the downfall of Numenor in *[[The Silmarillion]]* is one of the clearest mythic examples: he does not create the Numenoreans' fear of death, but redirects it into worship, conquest, and catastrophe.

Porky in *[[EarthBound]]* begins as a corrupted follower and becomes a corrupting ruler in *[[Mother 3]]*. His evil works by parodying authority, friendship, childhood, and safety.
