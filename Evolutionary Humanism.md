# Evolutionary Humanism

Evolutionary Humanism names Julian Huxley's attempt to understand humanity as evolution becoming conscious of itself. Human beings are not only products of evolution; they can, in this view, help direct future development through education, science, culture, and institutions.

## Humanity as Process

The concept treats humanity as unfinished. Culture, biology, education, and social organization become instruments through which the human species can develop further.

This connects directly to [[Conscious Evolution]] and [[Designed Successor Humanity]]. The key ambiguity is whether guided development serves human flourishing or turns humanity into a project managed by experts.

## Global Education

In *[[UNESCO - Its Purpose and Its Philosophy]]*, evolutionary humanism becomes institutional: education and culture are meant to form a more unified planetary consciousness.

This links the concept to [[World-Consciousness Formation]] and [[Global Problematique]]. The world problem requires not only policy, but the shaping of minds.
