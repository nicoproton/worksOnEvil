# False Cure

False Cure names stories where a system claims to heal suffering, disease, death, instability, or disorder, but the cure preserves control by damaging the goods that make life human. The cure may be medical, psychological, political, technological, spiritual, or ecological.

This bridge belongs under [[False Salvation Through Systems]]. The false cure is seductive because it answers a real wound. Its danger is not that suffering should never be healed, but that healing is offered by narrowing life until the wound no longer has to be felt.

Use this link when cure, treatment, preservation, purification, or therapeutic order becomes a means of dependency, selection, depersonalization, or domination.

In *[[Deus Ex]]*, Ambrosia answers the Gray Death while turning access to treatment into political leverage. In *[[Terranigma]]*, Beruga's immortality project preserves a chosen remnant by freezing ordinary life. In *[[Brave New World]]*, soma removes anguish by removing depth. In *[[We (Zamyatin)]]*, the Great Operation cures imagination by cutting away inward freedom. *[[Artificial Paradises]]* gives an earlier inward form: intoxication offers false ascent from spleen without healing the soul.

This concept overlaps with [[Artificial Immortality]] when the cure is survival from death, with [[Engineered Happiness]] when the cure is pleasure or emotional stability, and with [[Corrected Inwardness]] when the cure treats ordinary personhood as the disease.
