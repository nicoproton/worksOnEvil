# Predatory Immortality

Predatory Immortality names the attempt to defeat death by consuming the life of others. Unlike resurrection, rebirth, or transformed life, this immortality preserves the self by draining bodies, souls, blood, memory, youth, or psychic force.

The motif gives [[Artificial Immortality]] a moral edge. The problem is not only that life is extended unnaturally, but that extension requires another person's diminishment.

It is closely related to [[Privatio Boni]] when the predatory immortal does not generate life from within, but continues by taking life from a prior good. In that sense, vampirism can become a narrative image of evil as dependent, sterile, and parasitic.
