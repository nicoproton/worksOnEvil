# Blood Economy

Blood Economy names a social order organized around the extraction, circulation, scarcity, or management of life as fuel. In vampire stories this may be literal blood, but the same pattern can appear as psychic force, youth, memory, fertility, or spiritual vitality.

This concept differs from individual predation. It belongs where extraction becomes systemic: farms, feeding arrangements, cult tribute, medicalized harvesting, caste privilege, or communities that survive by normalizing the draining of others.

It overlaps with [[Stolen Life-Force Technology]] when extraction is mechanized, medicalized, or administered. It has a false-salvation aspect when the order presents itself as necessary peace, survival, or social stability.
