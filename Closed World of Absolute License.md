# Closed World of Absolute License

Closed World of Absolute License names the pattern in which an elite or sovereign group creates a sealed space where ordinary moral, familial, civic, or religious limits no longer apply. Inside the enclosure, persons are reduced to material for appetite, experiment, classification, punishment, or amusement.

This bridge belongs near [[Self-Imprisoning Domination]], [[Purification by Exclusion]], and [[Respectable Front]]. It differs from ordinary decadence because the enclosure is not only aesthetic or private. It becomes a small anti-world governed by permission without love.

Use this link when:

- a closed estate, institution, chamber, or inner circle suspends moral order;
- domination becomes ritualized, catalogued, bureaucratized, or staged;
- victims are excluded from the community of persons who count;
- the ruler's freedom depends on cutting the world off from witness, relation, and consequence.

*[[The 120 Days of Sodom]]* is the central Sadean case. It turns libertine desire into an enclosed system of absolute power. The concept can also clarify why some later technocratic or conspiratorial works feel Sadean even without erotic content: they imagine a managerial elite acting on human beings after removing every higher limit.
