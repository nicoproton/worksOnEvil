# Manichaeism and Zoroastrianism

**Manichaeism** and **Zoroastrianism** are distinct religious traditions, but both are often invoked in literary analysis when discussing cosmic dualism, especially conflict between light and darkness, spirit and matter, or creative and destructive powers.

This note should be used carefully. A work can resemble dualist imagery without directly deriving from either tradition. In the vault, the link is most useful when a story frames good and evil as opposed cosmic forces rather than as a privation or corruption within one created order.

*[[Dark Earth]]* is a useful example of this contrast. Its Light and Darkness imagery appears closer to opposed active forces than to the Augustinian or Tolkienian pattern of evil as privation.

*[[Terranigma]]* should be handled more cautiously. Light Gaia and Dark Gaia resemble cosmic dualism, but the game frames them less as doctrinal good and evil than as paired wills or faces of the Earth: life, technology, distortion, destruction, recurrence, and balance.
