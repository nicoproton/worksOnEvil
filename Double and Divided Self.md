# Double and Divided Self

Double and Divided Self names stories where a character encounters an alter ego, projected self, psychic double, or divided identity. The double may be literal, hallucinated, symbolic, or socially produced, but it matters because it reveals a fracture in personhood.

This concept is distinct from [[Artificial Woman as Double]]. Use that more specific node when the double is gendered as an artificial or woman-shaped image, idol, replacement, or manufactured desire-object.

Use this link when doubling exposes repression, obsession, divided agency, unstable identity, or a self that becomes alien to itself.

*[[The Picture of Dorian Gray]]* is one of the clearest cases: the portrait preserves Dorian's public beauty while becoming the visible form of his hidden corruption.

*[[Perfect Blue]]* gives the media-idol form: Mima's public image becomes a rival self produced by fandom, performance, online impersonation, and commercial memory.

The Lynch branch gives several ambiguous but strong cases. *[[Lost Highway]]* divides identity through guilt, fantasy, and recorded images. *[[Mulholland Drive]]* can be read as a Hollywood dream-self generated out of desire and failure. *[[Inland Empire]]* disperses the divided self through roles, rooms, performances, and screen layers. These works are especially close to [[Image-Generated Self]], where the double is produced through representation rather than appearing only as a psychological twin.
