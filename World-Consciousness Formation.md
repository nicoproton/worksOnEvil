# World-Consciousness Formation

World-Consciousness Formation names the attempt to create mental, moral, educational, and symbolic conditions for planetary order. It belongs under [[Engineered Consent]] when consent is not aimed at a single policy or product, but at a new scale of allegiance: world citizenship, global governance, evolutionary humanism, or planetary crisis consciousness.

Use this link when a work argues that global coordination requires the formation of new loyalties, values, language, education, or collective identity.

*[[The Open Conspiracy]]* gives the activist-intellectual form: Wells wants an openly declared movement of educated, scientific, internationalist actors to recruit minds for world civilization.

*[[The New World Order (Wells)]]* gives the institutional form: world government requires people to be educated away from national sovereignty and toward planetary civic belonging.

*[[UNESCO - Its Purpose and Its Philosophy]]* gives the educational-cultural form. Huxley imagines UNESCO as a means of forming minds for world unity and evolutionary humanism.

*[[The First Global Revolution]]* gives the crisis-governance form: planetary problems require planetary consciousness, but crisis can also become a symbolic instrument for mobilizing agreement.

*[[Practical Idealism]]* gives a European supranational form. Coudenhove-Kalergi's pacifism requires more than treaties: education, press, propaganda, and elite guidance must form a wider European consciousness strong enough to overcome nationalist war passions.

*[[The Externalisation of the Hierarchy]]* gives an occult-esoteric form: world consciousness is not only civic or institutional, but part of a hidden spiritual project for humanity.

Wagner's *[[The Artwork of the Future]]* gives an aesthetic form: shared consciousness is to be shaped through total art, myth, music, body, and communal participation rather than through policy alone.

This bridge should be used cautiously. It does not mean that every appeal to global cooperation is manipulative. It marks the graph's recurring question: when does necessary coordination become managed consciousness?
