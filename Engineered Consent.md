# Engineered Consent

Engineered Consent names the deliberate shaping of public agreement through media, symbols, experts, events, institutions, and group psychology. It is softer than coercion but can be more pervasive because people experience the resulting opinion as their own.

## Lippmann and Bernays

Walter Lippmann's *Public Opinion* gives the concept a diagnostic form through the "manufacture of consent." Edward Bernays gives it a practical form in *Propaganda* and *Crystallizing Public Opinion*, where public relations becomes a technique for forming attitudes.

## Consent as System

The concept connects to [[Engineered Happiness]] but is not identical. Engineered happiness shapes desire and emotional stability; engineered consent shapes public agreement and political legitimacy.

Use this link when persuasion is organized through symbols, media, group identity, or expert mediation rather than direct force.

The world-state and global-governance branch depends heavily on this concept. *The Open Conspiracy*, *The New World Order*, *UNESCO - Its Purpose and Its Philosophy*, and *The First Global Revolution* all require some formation of public consciousness beyond ordinary national politics.

The classical form is [[Noble Lie]], where political myth stabilizes the city by shaping how citizens understand their own roles.
