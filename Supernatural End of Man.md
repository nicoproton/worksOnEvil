# Supernatural End of Man

Supernatural End of Man names the Catholic claim that man is not fulfilled by a merely natural, political, biological, economic, psychological, or historical completion. Human nature is real and good, but man is called beyond himself to God by grace.

This bridge belongs near [[Personalist Common Good]], [[Reason Ordered to Truth]], and [[False Salvation Through Systems]]. It is useful when a text explains why secular humanism, technocratic care, revolutionary politics, or therapeutic adjustment cannot finally save man: they aim too low.

Use this link when:

- man is treated as ordered to God rather than only to nature, society, history, production, or self-expression;
- a system promises to fulfill man while excluding grace, transcendence, or beatitude;
- the natural good of the person is affirmed but shown to be incomplete without supernatural destiny;
- Catholic thought distinguishes man's dignity from both individualism and collective absorption.

Henri de Lubac's *[[Surnaturel]]* is the central theological anchor. Its controversy matters: de Lubac was not writing an encyclical, and his account of nature and grace was disputed. But the graph-useful point is clear: if man is made for God, then no closed natural order can provide his final meaning.

De Lubac's *[[The Drama of Atheist Humanism]]* gives the negative modern form. Atheistic humanism tries to recover man by removing God, but the result is a reduced man.

Maritain's *[[Integral Humanism]]* gives the political-humanist form. A humanism that refuses man's spiritual end becomes partial and can become anti-human while still speaking in man's name.

*[[Spe Salvi]]* gives the encyclical form through hope. Science, structures, and politics can serve human life, but they cannot redeem man from outside or replace the supernatural horizon of salvation.

Etienne Gilson's [[Christian Philosophy]] gives a philosophical-historical neighbor. Revelation helps reason remember that man is not adequately explained by a closed natural order.

This concept should not be used for every religious text. It belongs where the difference between natural fulfillment and supernatural destiny is doing real interpretive work.
