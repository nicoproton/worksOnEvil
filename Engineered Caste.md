# Engineered Caste

Engineered Caste names the pattern in which human beings are produced, conditioned, educated, or biologically shaped for predetermined social roles. The issue is not only inequality after birth, but formation before genuine freedom can appear.

This bridge belongs under [[Manufactured Humanity]] when reproduction and development are treated as instruments of social design.

Use this link when a work focuses on caste, class, breeding, cloning, conditioning, or education as a way of making persons fit a system.

*[[Brave New World]]* is the clearest fictional case: hatcheries, chemical conditioning, hypnopaedia, and caste sorting make social function prior to personhood.

*[[Apollo's Song]]* gives the pattern a Tezuka variation through cloned rule, emotional incapacity, and the conflict between manufactured hierarchy and love.

Non-fiction and utopian works can belong here more cautiously. *[[The Scientific Outlook]]* and *[[New Bottles for New Wine]]* matter because they treat future human formation as a field for scientific, educational, or evolutionary planning.
