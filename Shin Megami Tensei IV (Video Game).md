# Shin Megami Tensei IV (Video Game)

***Shin Megami Tensei IV*** is a 2013 role-playing game for the Nintendo 3DS. It is set in a post-apocalyptic world where the technologically advanced kingdom of Mikado exists beneath a protective dome, believing itself to be the sole bastion of humanity, while a demon-infested, ruined Tokyo lies beneath. The game explores themes of social stratification, engineered reality, and the struggle between order and chaos, often manipulated by powerful, unseen forces.

## Black Iron Prison & Layered Conspiracy

The kingdom of Mikado is a literal [[Black Iron Prison]]. Its citizens live under a dome, protected by angels, and are kept ignorant of the outside world and their own history. This engineered isolation and the manipulation of information by the ruling elite and angelic forces perfectly embody a [[Layered Conspiracy]] designed to maintain a specific social order and prevent dissent.

## False Salvation Through Systems

The angelic faction, representing the "Law" alignment, offers a vision of salvation that involves strict control, the elimination of "impure" elements, and a rigid social hierarchy. This presents a clear example of [[False Salvation Through Systems]], where a promised utopia comes at the cost of individual freedom and truth, enforced by a powerful, dogmatic system.

## Engineered Consent

The populace of Mikado is conditioned from birth to accept their roles and the divine authority of the angels. Their history is rewritten, and their understanding of the world is carefully managed, leading to a profound [[Engineered Consent]] to their own confinement and manipulation. This control extends to their beliefs and aspirations, making rebellion almost unthinkable for many.

## Technological Dystopia & Fallen High Civilization

The world outside Mikado is a ruined, demon-haunted Tokyo, a stark image of a [[Fallen High Civilization]]. Mikado itself, while seemingly pristine, is a technologically maintained, isolated society built upon the ashes of a past catastrophe, functioning as a unique form of [[Technological Dystopia]] where technology is used to create a false paradise and enforce a rigid social order.

## Cosmic Axis Under Siege

The overarching conflict between the Law and Chaos factions, and the protagonist's choices, directly determine the fate of humanity and the nature of the new world. This struggle over fundamental principles and the very fabric of existence, where different ideologies vie to reshape reality, aligns with the concept of a [[Cosmic Axis Under Siege]].
