# Total Technical Environment

Total Technical Environment names works where technology is no longer a tool inside the world, but the world-like condition in which people live. Architecture, interfaces, schedules, access systems, machines, and infrastructure become the practical environment of human existence.

This bridge belongs under [[Technological Dystopia]], but it is more precise than the parent concept. The emphasis is not simply that technology controls people, but that technological mediation has replaced ordinary dwelling, bodily relation, privacy, landscape, or shared reality.

Use this link when a work's dystopia is environmental rather than merely instrumental: the system surrounds life so completely that people must inhabit it before they can judge it.

*[[The Machine Stops]]* is an early pure case. The Machine supplies rooms, air, food, conversation, music, and knowledge until embodied life outside mediation becomes almost unthinkable.

*[[BLAME!]]* gives the motif its architectural extreme. The City has become an autonomous megastructure whose systems continue after human command has collapsed.

*[[We (Zamyatin)]]* gives the motif a rationalist and political form: glass architecture, synchronized time, and mathematical order make the state visible in the shape of everyday life.

*[[Metropolis (Novel)]]* is a symbolic-industrial case. The city is not just a backdrop for class conflict; it is a machine-age spiritual architecture built as a modern Babel.
